How to Make Custom Emojis from Your Photos

Turning a photo into a custom emoji is one of the most popular things people do with EmojiAI. Here's a step-by-step guide to getting the best results.

1. Choose your photo

Almost any photo works, but the best results come from images with a clear subject. A portrait with good lighting, a pet photo where the face is visible, or a picture of an object with distinct edges will all produce cleaner emojis. Avoid busy backgrounds if you can — the AI handles them fine, but a clean subject gives you more control.

2. Upload and let the AI process

Open EmojiAI, tap "Create from Photo," and select your image. The app will analyze the photo and identify the main subject. You don't need to crop or edit anything beforehand — the AI handles the extraction.

3. Pick a style

This is where the magic happens. The same photo can produce completely different results depending on the style you choose:

  • 3D — Turns your photo into a dimensional, Pixar-like character
  • Emoji — Classic flat emoji style, perfect for stickers and reactions
  • Anime — Manga-inspired look with bold lines and expressive features
  • Sticker — Cutout style with a white border, ready to share
  • Pixel — Retro 8-bit game aesthetic
  • Watercolor — Soft painted look with gentle gradients

Try a few styles on the same photo — each one gives you a different take on the same subject.

4. Generate and save

Once you're happy with the result, save it to your collection. EmojiAI stores everything you make in History, so you can find it later. You can also organize your favorites into named collections.

Tips for better results

For portraits, front-facing photos with both eyes visible work best. For objects, isolate the item against a plain surface. And don't be afraid to experiment — some of the best emojis come from unexpected photos.
