## Step 1 — Open EmojiAI and Choose "From Photo"

Launch the EmojiAI app and tap **From Photo** on the home screen.

![EmojiAI Home Screen](/assets/blog/1-choose-from-photo.webp)

This option allows you to upload an existing image or take a new photo using your camera.

---

## Step 2 — Upload Your Photo

Select a clear photo from your gallery or capture a new one.

![Upload Photo](/assets/blog/2-select-photo.webp)

For the best results:

- Use a clear, well-lit photo.
- Keep the face centered.
- Avoid heavy filters.
- Make sure the face isn't covered by objects.

## Tips for Better Results

To get the highest-quality emoji:

- 📸 Use high-resolution photos.
- 😊 Natural facial expressions work best.
- 💡 Bright lighting improves AI accuracy.
- 👤 One person per photo is recommended.
- ✂️ Avoid heavily cropped faces.
