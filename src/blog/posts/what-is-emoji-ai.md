EmojiAI lets you create custom emojis from text descriptions or photos. Instead of scrolling through a keyboard of predefined emojis trying to find something close, you describe exactly what you want — and the AI generates it.

## How it works

Open the app, type a prompt like "a corgi wearing sunglasses riding a skateboard" — or upload a photo of anything around you — pick a style (3D, Anime, Sticker, Pixel, or the classic Emoji look), and EmojiAI generates your custom emoji in seconds.

## Why use EmojiAI?

Standard emojis give you a fixed set of options. If you want something specific — your pet as an emoji, an idea that doesn't have an existing character, a consistent style for a project — you're out of luck. EmojiAI fills that gap. Every generated emoji is unique to what you asked for, and the style is always consistent because you choose it before generating.

## What people are making

Users have generated everything from personal profile pics and reaction stickers to custom icons for notes and social content. The same prompt with different styles produces completely different results, so there's room to experiment.
